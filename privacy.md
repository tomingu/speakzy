# Privacy Policy for Speakzy

Last updated: September 2026

Speakzy ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data handling.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
Speakzy does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). 

### 2. Microphone Access & Audio Recordings
The app requests access to your device's microphone solely to allow you to record your MUET speaking practice sessions. 
- All voice recordings remain strictly local on your device.
- We do not upload, transmit, or stream audio files to any external servers or third parties.
- You can delete your recordings or revoke microphone access at any time through your iOS device settings.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The app does not use third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

### 4. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub:
https://github.com/tomingu/speakzy
